The Mission

Conversations Beyond The Stratosphere

Out of Orbit is a late-night talk show produced and broadcast live from the studios of WMTU 91.9 FM, the student-run radio voice of Michigan Technological University in Houghton, Michigan.

Airing every Friday night from 10:00 PM to 12:00 AM EST, the show delves into speculative science, space exploration, weird technology, music production, and engaging open discussions across the airwaves and online streams.
