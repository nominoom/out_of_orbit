The Host & Format

Anything, Everything & Weekly Music Themes

Hosted by Aurora, Out of Orbit is an open-format cosmic late-night talk show broadcast live from the studios of WMTU 91.9 FM in Houghton, Michigan.

Aurora talks about anything and everything—from deep astrophysics and obscure technology anomalies to internet culture, campus lore, and candid midnight philosophical rabbit holes. Each episode is soundtracked by a specially curated weekly music theme that evolves with every transmission. You can catch the soundtrack in real time by tuning in on the 91.9 FM airwaves or hopping onto the Twitch livestream.
